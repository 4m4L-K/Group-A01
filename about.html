About the Website

This project is a biography website dedicated to Lionel Messi — one of the most celebrated football players in the world. The website aims to provide a more immersive and interactive alternative to traditional biography formats such as written articles or standard informational webpages.

Our Vision

Designed with a cinematic and user-focused approach, the website combines visual storytelling, animations, and interactive elements to enhance user engagement and create a more memorable browsing experience.

The design and presentation are inspired by the modern aesthetics of the Grand Theft Auto VI promotional website and the personal website of Lando Norris.
